What is a PDF Compiler & Merger?

A PDF compiler and merger is a tool that lets you combine two or more PDF documents into a single cohesive file. Whether you're compiling a project report, combining scanned invoices, or assembling a research portfolio, PDFMaster's free online PDF merger does it all — instantly and privately in your browser.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDFMaster's Merge PDF completely free?▾
Yes. PDFMaster is 100% free with no hidden fees, no premium plan required, and no limit on the number of PDFs you can merge.
Are my PDF files secure when using this tool?▾
Absolutely. All PDF processing happens entirely on your device using browser-native JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.
How many PDF files can I merge at once?▾
There is no hard limit on the number of files. However, very large batches (e.g. 50+ large PDFs) may be constrained by your device's available RAM. For best performance, merge in batches of 20–30 files if needed.
Can I select specific pages from each PDF?▾
Yes! Each uploaded file has a "From page" and "To page" input. Enter your desired range and only those pages will be included in the merged output. Leave blank to include all pages.
Does the tool work on mobile and tablets?▾
Yes. PDFMaster is fully responsive and works on all modern mobile browsers including Chrome on Android and Safari on iOS.
Can I merge password-protected PDFs?▾
No. Password-protected or encrypted PDFs cannot be merged. You'll need to remove the password from your PDFs first before uploading them to this tool.
Will the quality of my PDFs degrade after merging?▾
No. PDFMaster uses pdf-lib, which merges PDFs at the document structure level — preserving fonts, images, vector graphics, and text at their original quality.
