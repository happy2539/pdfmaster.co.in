What is a PDF Merger & Combiner?

A PDF merger is a powerful online tool that allows you to combine two or more PDF files into a single document. Whether you need to merge reports, combine scanned receipts, or join PDF pages for a portfolio, PDFMaster provides the fastest and most secure way to merge PDF online—completely free and without any file uploads.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDFMaster's Merge PDF completely free? ▾
Yes. PDFMaster is 100% free with no hidden fees, no premium plan required, and no limit on the number of PDFs you can merge.
Why does PDFMaster work so smoothly even with large PDF files? ▾
Unlike ordinary web tools that load all files into system RAM at once and crash your browser tab, PDFMaster features an intelligent Tiered Low-RAM Architecture. When the combined size of your selected files exceeds 50 MB, raw PDF data is cached directly into your browser's local IndexedDB disk storage instead of overloading your system RAM. During compilation, each file is loaded, pages are extracted, and memory is immediately reclaimed before reading the next file. This guarantees silky-smooth operation, real-time progress feedback, and zero browser crashes even when compiling hundreds of megabytes of documents.
Are my PDF files secure when using this tool? ▾
Absolutely. All PDF processing happens entirely on your device using browser-native JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.
How many PDF files can I merge at once? ▾
There is no hard limit on the number of files. PDFMaster's Low-RAM architecture streams documents sequentially from local storage, allowing you to merge large batches of files smoothly without exhausting your system memory.
Can I select specific pages from each PDF? ▾
Yes! Each uploaded file has a "From page" and "To page" input. Enter your desired range and only those pages will be included in the merged output. Leave blank to include all pages.
Does the tool work on mobile and tablets? ▾
Yes. PDFMaster is fully responsive and works on all modern mobile browsers including Chrome on Android and Safari on iOS.
Can I restore my last merged files if I accidentally close the tab? ▾
Yes. PDFMaster includes a built-in recovery mode powered by your browser's local IndexedDB storage. Your uploaded files, sequence order, and page ranges are saved 100% locally on your device. If your browser unexpectedly closes, simply click the Recovery icon in the header to resume right where you left off. This saves your precious time, requires no re-uploading, and is completely free without paying any hard-earned money.
