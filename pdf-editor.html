What Is It

A PDF editor that runs entirely on your device

PDFMaster's PDF Editor lets you mark up and edit any PDF right inside your browser. Add text, shapes, highlights, images, or custom signatures, then export a clean new file — without your document ever leaving your computer or phone.

There's no upload step, so there's nothing to wait on and nothing sitting on a server in the meantime. Open a file, mark it up, and download the result in the same tab — it works the same way on a laptop at your desk or a phone on the train.

Features

Everything you need to mark up a PDF

Add text

Drop in labels, notes, or answers anywhere on the page, in the size and color you choose. Customize text formatting with bold, italic, and underline options.

Draw & highlight

Sketch freehand with the pen, or sweep a highlighter over important sections. Pick any shade using our color palette, custom picker, or eyedropper.

Shapes & arrows

Box something in, circle it, or point straight at it with rectangles, ellipses, lines, and arrows. Adjust stroke thickness to fit your needs.

Images & stamps

Insert your own logo, photo, or approval stamp, then crop and resize it perfectly right on the page.

Sign documents

Draw a signature, type your name using elegant cursive script fonts, or upload an image and use WASM AI to instantly remove the background.

Export instantly

Save your marked-up file as a new PDF in one tap. The original pages stay untouched underneath.
