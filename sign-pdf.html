Overview

What is PDFMaster Sign PDF?

A fast, private, and legally binding client-side electronic signature platform designed for individuals, legal teams, and enterprises.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Unlike legacy cloud signers that store your sensitive contracts and tax forms on external servers, PDFMaster processes every byte locally in your web browser. Your private documents never leave your device.

Legally Binding & Compliant

Electronic signatures produced comply fully with the United States ESIGN Act, UETA, EU eIDAS Article 25(1), and UK regulations for standard electronic signatures (SES), creating enforceable agreements.

Zero Latency, Low-RAM Architecture

Built with tiered canvas memory management and WebAssembly. Even on 100+ page contracts and memory-constrained mobile devices, pages render smoothly with zero browser lag.

Compliance

Legal Validity Around the Globe

Learn how PDFMaster aligns with global electronic signature statutes and compliance frameworks.

Under major electronic commerce laws worldwide, standard electronic signatures (SES) carry the same legal weight and admissibility in court as wet-ink pen signatures. PDFMaster captures intent, associates the signature image indelibly into the PDF content stream, and preserves document integrity.

🇺🇸 United States (ESIGN & UETA)

The Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ESIGN, 15 U.S.C. § 7001) and Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA) confirm that a contract or signature may not be denied legal effect solely because it is in electronic form.

🇪🇺 European Union (eIDAS Regulation)

Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS) Article 25(1) establishes that an electronic signature shall not be denied legal effect and admissibility as evidence in legal proceedings solely on the grounds that it is in electronic format.

🇬🇧 UK & 🇮🇳 Global Statutes

Recognized under the UK Electronic Communications Act 2000, India’s Information Technology Act 2000 (Section 5), Canada’s PIPEDA, and Australia’s Electronic Transactions Act 1999 for routine commercial agreements.

Engineering

Low-RAM & High-Speed Architecture

How PDFMaster delivers instant, memory-efficient PDF signing inside the browser sandbox.

Virtual Viewport Rendering

Multi-page PDFs do not bloat heap memory. PDFMaster isolates rendering to the active viewport canvas using IntersectionObserver and cancels in-flight tasks when you jump between pages.

In-Place Vector Stamping

Unlike basic tools that convert entire PDF pages to low-res JPEG images, PDFMaster injects PNG signature objects directly into the original PDF content stream, preserving all vector text and links.

IndexedDB Auto-Recovery

Your progress is auto-saved locally using IndexedDB binary key-value storage. If your laptop battery dies or your browser tab crashes, one click restores your document and signature placements.
