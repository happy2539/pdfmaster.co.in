Overview

What is PDF Page Numbering?

A fast, private browser utility engineered to add clean, professional headers, footers, pagination, and Bates numbering to any PDF document.

When combining research papers, preparing legal exhibits, compiling company financial statements, or publishing eBooks, documents often lack uniform pagination. A dedicated PDF page numbering tool stamps sequential numbers, folio headers, or page count ratios (e.g., "Page 1 of 45") onto every sheet with millimeter accuracy.

Unlike conventional online utilities that require uploading your confidential files to external cloud servers, PDFMaster executes 100% locally in your browser sandbox. Powered by high-performance WebAssembly engines (pdf-lib and PDF.js), your sensitive agreements, student theses, and medical records never leave your device.

With support for facing pages (duplex mode for double-sided printing), cover page skipping, custom text templates, Roman numerals, and background badges for dark scans, pagination is effortless and flawless.

Technology

High-Efficiency Low-RAM Architecture

Built on streaming range readers, vector font embedding, and recycled GPU canvas buffers.

Zero-Heap Blob Streaming

PDF bytes are streamed via sliced Object URLs rather than holding huge uncompressed ArrayBuffers in memory. This eliminates GC thrashing and supports multi-hundred megabyte documents.

Recycled Canvas Pools

Thumbnail rendering reuses a fixed pool of 3 GPU canvases. As soon as a thumbnail is converted into a lightweight image, the canvas context is deallocated immediately.

IntersectionObserver Virtualization

Only thumbnails currently visible in your browser window are rendered. Offscreen pages consume near-zero memory until scrolled into view.

Direct Vector Stamping

Numbers are embedded as native PDF text operators without rasterizing document pages. File size increase is negligible (<10KB) and zoom quality remains pin-sharp.
