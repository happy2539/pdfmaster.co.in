Comparison Modes

Engineered Inspection Capabilities

Different document workflows require different comparison techniques. PDFMaster gives you precision diff modes and tools in one unified studio.

1. Side-by-Side Split View

Displays Document A and Document B side-by-side with synchronized scrolling and zoom. When you pan or zoom in one pane, the other tracks in lockstep, making manual review natural and effortless.

2. Visual Diff (Pixel Overlay)

Superimposes both pages and renders pixel deltas with smart color coding: deleted content is highlighted in vibrant Red, additions in Green, and identical context in low-contrast grayscale.

3. Rapid Flicker A/B Mode

Alternates rapidly between Document A and Document B right in your viewport. Because the human eye detects motion instantly, subtle margin slips, font shifts, or nudge edits immediately pop out.

4. Change Inspector & Audit PDF

Browse page-by-page revision statuses in the interactive side drawer, jump straight to modified sheets, and download a comprehensive Executive Comparison Audit Report with one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About PDF Compare

Got questions? Here are detailed answers on how PDFMaster compares documents safely and accurately.

How does PDFMaster compare two PDF files?
PDFMaster uses client-side WebAssembly and canvas rendering engines. It parses text content using PDF.js to perform an LCS (Longest Common Subsequence) word-level comparison while simultaneously rendering high-DPI raster layers to compute a pixel-by-pixel visual difference overlay. Any text or layout differences are highlighted with Red (deletions) and Green (additions).
Are my confidential contracts or NDA documents safe?
Yes, absolutely 100%. PDFMaster runs entirely in your local browser sandbox. Your PDF files, extracted text, and rendered comparison pages never leave your device and are never transmitted over the internet to any server. You can even disconnect your internet connection or use Airplane Mode and the tool will continue working flawlessly.
What happens if the two PDFs have different numbers of pages?
PDFMaster handles documents of unequal lengths seamlessly. The total comparison count adapts to the maximum number of pages between both documents. Pages that exist in Document B but not Document A are flagged as added pages in the Changes Inspector drawer.
Can I export a PDF comparison report to share with colleagues or clients?
Yes! Simply click the "Export Audit Report (PDF)" button in the lower toolbar. PDFMaster will compile a professional Executive Comparison Audit Report containing timestamps, file metadata, similarity percentages, and a page-by-page change breakdown table.
What is the difference between Side-by-Side view and Visual Diff?
Side-by-Side view presents Document A (Baseline) and Document B (Modified) alongside each other with synchronized scrolling and zoom controls, allowing you to read both versions in parallel. Visual Diff superimposes both documents on top of each other and highlights pixel changes: additions appear in green, deletions in red, and identical sections are rendered in low contrast. You can also switch between Highlight Clouds, Delta Mask, and Rapid Flicker submodes.
Does PDFMaster support password-protected PDF files?
Yes. If either Document A or Document B has password encryption, PDFMaster will prompt you for the decryption passphrase and unlock it locally in memory using WebAssembly. Alternatively, you can use our dedicated Unlock PDF tool to permanently remove passwords.
