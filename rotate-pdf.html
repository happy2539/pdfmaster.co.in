Overview

What is a PDF Page Rotator?

A fast, private utility engineered to permanently correct upside-down, sideways, or misoriented pages across any PDF file.

When digitizing paper documents with mobile scanners, combining multi-page contracts, or exporting spreadsheets alongside slides, pages frequently end up sideways or upside down. A dedicated PDF page rotator enables you to inspect every sheet visually, flip misaligned pages into the correct orientation, and save a crisp, publication-ready PDF in seconds.

Unlike conventional online utilities that require uploading your private documents to third-party cloud servers, PDFMaster executes 100% locally in your browser. Using high-performance WebAssembly engines (pdf-lib and PDF.js), your confidential invoices, legal agreements, and personal notes remain entirely on your device.

With support for individual page rotations, batch selection (odd/even/custom ranges), automatic portrait and landscape alignment, and undo/redo history, fixing PDF orientations has never been faster or safer.

Key Capabilities

Engineered for Precision & Complete Privacy

Cutting-edge client-side browser architecture ensures seamless rotations without quality loss, limits, or security risks.

Permanent Lossless Rotation

Updates the official PDF specification /Rotate attribute directly in the document catalog. No raster recompression, zero pixel distortion, and instant file generation.

Smart Orientation Auto-Alignment

Detects mismatched orientations across multi-page scans and lets you align "All to Portrait" or "All to Landscape" with a single click.

Tiered Low-RAM Engine

Employs range chunk streaming and virtualized thumbnail rendering with automatic GPU canvas deallocation to handle massive multi-hundred-page documents without crashing your browser.

Smart Session Recovery

IndexedDB local storage safeguards your document and rotation state. If your browser crashes or reloads, restore your exact workspace with one click.

Undo / Redo & Shortcuts

Experiment freely with full historical rollback. Use intuitive keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) and individual page reset toggles.
