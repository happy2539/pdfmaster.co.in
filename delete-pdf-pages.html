Overview

What is a PDF Page Deletor?

A specialized PDF management tool designed to cleanly excise unnecessary pages, blank sheets, confidential sections, or outdated addenda from any multi-page PDF document.

When scanning documents, exporting presentation slides, or receiving large multi-chapter contracts, you often end up with duplicate sheets, accidental blank pages, cover slides, or private annexures that shouldn't be shared. A PDF page deletor lets you inspect each sheet visually, strike out unwanted content, and export a polished, clean file in seconds.

Unlike conventional web utilities that require uploading your sensitive PDFs to remote cloud servers, PDFMaster's PDF Page Deletor executes 100% locally on your machine. Built with modern browser WebAssembly and JavaScript engines (pdf-lib and PDF.js), your files never leave your device's memory.

With support for batch selections, custom number ranges (e.g. 2, 5-8, 12), odd/even filtering, and undo/redo history, cleaning up PDF documents has never been faster, safer, or more intuitive.

Key Capabilities

Engineered for Performance & Privacy

Cutting-edge browser architecture ensures you can edit massive documents without crashes or privacy risks.

Tiered Low-RAM Architecture

When documents exceed 50 MB, raw PDF buffers are offloaded directly into local IndexedDB disk storage. Thumbnails render sequentially with active garbage collection to prevent browser tab freezing.

Smart Session Recovery

Accidentally closed your browser or lost power? PDFMaster automatically caches your active document and deletion states in IndexedDB. Click the recovery icon in the navbar to resume immediately.

Undo & Redo History

Mistakes happen. Every single deletion toggle, batch action, and range deletion is recorded in a 30-level history stack. Undo with a click or press Ctrl+Z anytime.

Multi-Range Syntax Deletion

Have a list of specific pages to purge? Simply type 1, 4-7, 12, 19 into the range input bar to instantly mark multiple non-contiguous pages for deletion with a single tap.

100% Private Client-Side Engine

Your sensitive legal documents, medical forms, and bank statements remain strictly confidential. No uploads, no cloud queues, no tracking, and no third-party data transmission.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deleting pages from PDF files with PDFMaster.

PDFMaster leverages modern browser technologies including PDF.js (developed by Mozilla) for rendering visual page canvases and pdf-lib for binary PDF manipulation. When you upload a document, it is read into your browser's local sandbox memory. Pages flagged for deletion are omitted while all preserved pages are compiled into a fresh PDF file directly on your computer or smartphone.

Standard web tools crash when handling PDFs over 50MB because browsers enforce strict memory limits per tab. PDFMaster solves this by using a Tiered Low-RAM Architecture.

When your PDF exceeds 50MB, the master byte buffer is moved into your browser's persistent IndexedDB storage, freeing active RAM immediately. Thumbnail rendering is throttled with micro-yields, and during document export, data is streamed on demand. This guarantees fast performance and zero tab crashes.

As you interact with the tool, your document binary data, filename, and marked page selections are automatically synced to an IndexedDB store named pdfmaster_delete_db.

If you accidentally close your tab or refresh the page, PDFMaster detects the saved session and displays a flashing recovery indicator on the top navigation bar, along with a prompt to restore your exact workspace with one click.
