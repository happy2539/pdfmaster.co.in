🐌 Why Dedicated PNG to PDF Apps Frustrate Mobile Users

Most dedicated PDF converter apps on Android and iPhone follow the same frustrating pattern, regardless of their Play Store or App Store ratings:

  1. Cold start delay. Even "lightweight" PNG to PDF apps take 2–5 seconds to launch from scratch. If you haven't used the app recently, it may need to fully reload before you've selected a single PNG file.
  2. Permission walls. Apps frequently request camera, gallery, or storage access on first run — adding friction before any conversion takes place.
  3. Freemium gates. Many apps allow one free PNG to PDF conversion, then prompt an upgrade to merge multiple PNG images into one PDF — which is the most common use case.
