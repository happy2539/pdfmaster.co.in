What Is It

A free PDF viewer that runs entirely on your device

PDFMaster's PDF Viewer lets you open and read any PDF file instantly, right inside your browser. It's powered by Mozilla's open-source PDF.js rendering engine, running locally on your own device — not on our servers.

There's no upload step, so there's nothing to wait on and nothing sitting on a server in the meantime. Open a report, ebook, scanned document, contract, or form, and it renders in the same tab — on a laptop at your desk or a phone on the train, exactly the same way.

Because everything happens on-device, this PDF viewer online tool also works without an internet connection once the page has loaded, and it can safely open password-protected files without ever sending your password anywhere.

Features

Everything you need to read a PDF properly

Instant page navigation

Jump between pages with Previous and Next controls, or type a page number to go straight there.

Smooth zoom & fit width

Zoom in for fine print, zoom out for a full-page view, or fit the page to your screen with a single click.

Rotate pages

Fix a sideways-scanned page instantly, right in the viewer — no re-uploading or re-scanning required.

Search inside the PDF

Find any word or phrase across the whole document, with matches highlighted and one-click jumps between results.

Select & copy text

Select and copy text straight out of the PDF, exactly like a native reader — no extra conversion step.

100% private processing

Your file is decoded and rendered locally in your browser's memory. It's never uploaded, stored, or seen by our servers.

Smart session recovery

Pick up right where you left off. Your reading page, zoom level, and document are saved locally for instant one-click recovery.

3-Page Low-RAM Cache

Loads entire files in IndexedDB disk storage while maintaining only a 3-page sliding window (current - 1, current, current + 1) in RAM for instant, lag-free page navigation.
