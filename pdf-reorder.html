What is it

What is a PDF Reorder Tool?

A PDF page reorder tool lets you rearrange the pages of any PDF document into any order you need — all without downloading desktop software or uploading to a server.

  • Instantly rearrange PDF pages for reports, presentations, books, and contracts that were scanned or compiled in the wrong order.
  • Remove unwanted pages before sharing — keep only what matters.
  • Preview each page as a visual thumbnail so you always know exactly what you're moving.
  • Works entirely in your browser — no installation, no account, no file uploads ever required.

How to Use

Reorder PDF Pages in 4 Easy Steps

Go from messy to perfectly ordered in under a minute.

1

Upload Your PDF

Click "Choose PDF File" or drag and drop your PDF directly onto the upload zone. Your file is read locally — nothing is sent anywhere.

Tip: Works with any PDF regardless of how it was created
2

Preview All Pages

All pages render as visual thumbnails, numbered in their current order. Scan through to identify exactly which pages need to move.

3

Drag to Rearrange (or Use Tools)

Drag any page thumbnail to a new position. Use "Reverse" to flip the entire order. Click the delete button to remove pages you don't need.

Touch-friendly — works on mobile and tablets too
4

Download Your Reordered PDF

Click "Download PDF" to save the reorganized file to your device. The output is a fully compatible PDF with pages in your new order.
