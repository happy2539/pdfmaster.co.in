About

What is Photo to PDF Converter?

A free, browser-based tool that converts your images files into a downloadable PDF — no account, no uploads, no waiting.

Whether you need to combine photos for a presentation, create a portfolio PDF, or archive your images in a universal format, PDFMaster's Image to PDF tool handles it all in seconds. It supports all major image formats including JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, GIF, TIFF, SVG and AVIF. Advanced options like custom page sizes, orientation control and drag-to-reorder give you full control over the final document — all without leaving your browser.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here are the most common ones answered.

Can I recover my images and settings if my browser closes?

+
Yes! PDFMaster features automatic session recovery powered by your browser's private IndexedDB storage. Your selected images, page ordering, and PDF options (size, orientation, compression) are safely preserved 100% locally on your machine. If your browser or tab closes, simply click the Recovery button in the top header or the restore notification prompt to resume your work instantly without re-uploading.

Is my image data safe? Are files uploaded to a server?

+
No. All conversion happens entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. Your images are never uploaded, transmitted or stored on any server. You can even use the tool offline after the page loads.

What image formats are supported?

+
The tool supports JPG/JPEG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, GIF, TIFF/TIF, SVG and AVIF. Any image format your browser can display is supported.

Is there a limit on how many images I can convert?

+
There is no enforced limit. However, converting very large batches of high-resolution images may slow down your browser depending on available RAM. Batches under 50 images are recommended for smooth performance.

Can I control the order of images in the PDF?

+
Yes. After selecting your images, thumbnail previews will appear. Simply drag and drop them to rearrange the order before converting. Each thumbnail is numbered to show the current page order.

Will the PDF have a watermark?

+
No. PDFMaster is completely free and produces clean, watermark-free PDFs. No account or payment is ever required.

Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?

+
Yes. The tool is fully responsive and tested on iOS Safari and Android Chrome. You can select images from your camera roll or device storage directly.
